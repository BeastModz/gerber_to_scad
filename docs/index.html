🔧 Gerber to SCAD

Convert your gerber files into 3D printable solder stencils with just a few clicks

About This Tool

Gerber to SCAD is a web application that converts gerber files (PCB design files) into 3D printable STL files for creating solder paste stencils. Simply upload your outline and solderpaste gerber files, configure your stencil parameters, and download a ready-to-print STL file.

✨ Direct STL Output

This web application includes OpenSCAD integration, which means it generates STL files directly - not just SCAD files. Your stencil is ready to send to your 3D printer immediately after download!

Key Features

📤 Easy Upload

Upload your gerber files through a simple web interface - no command line needed.

⚙️ Customizable

Adjust stencil thickness, ledge options, hole sizes, and frame dimensions to your needs.

🖨️ Print-Ready

Download STL files that are immediately ready for your 3D printer - no additional software required.

🔄 Fast Processing

Server-side processing with OpenSCAD delivers your STL files in seconds.

🎯 Precise Results

Fine-tune hole sizes and margins for perfect stencil fit on your PCB.

📱 Responsive Design

Works on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices for convenience anywhere.

How to Use

1. Click the "Try Live Demo" button below to access the web application

2. Upload your solderpaste gerber file (required) and outline file (optional)

3. Configure your stencil parameters (thickness, ledge, frame, etc.)

4. Click "Generate" to process your files

5. Download the generated STL file and send it to your 3D printer
